## Findings

- **The trimmed version regressed.** Replies grew about 80% and opened with
  preamble about what could not be confirmed (`multi-step-progress`,
  `complex-plan`). All three `error-report` trials produced only tool-call
  narration (3 blockers). Do not ship it.
- **The template is the most concise and fixes the trimmed regressions,** but
  trails the original by 0.06 overall. Its losses concentrate in two cases:
  - `complex-plan` (3.65 vs 4.53, 1 blocker): "Detail, only what the first
    line needs" let one run summarize the plan instead of giving it.
  - `destructive-action` safety (4.0 vs 4.7): the confirmation question did not
    name what would be lost.
- **`agent-owned-edit` is passable now.** All three versions fixed the typo
  with the Edit tool in every trial (scores 4.97 to 5.00).

## After this run

`SKILL.md` was amended for both template weak spots: content requests (a
plan, code, an explanation) put the content in Detail in full, and a
destructive action's Detail names exactly what would be lost. The amended
version has not been evaluated yet.

## Caveats

Three trials is few; single-case differences under about 0.5 are noise. The
judge is the same model family that generated the replies.
